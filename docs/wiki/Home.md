# Welcome to the RookReader Wiki!

RookReader is a modern, fast, cross-platform application for viewing e-books and comics.   
It seamlessly supports comic archives (zip, cbz, rar, cbr) — including the folders inside them — PDF documents, EPUB files, and raw image sequences contained within directories.  
The application is specifically designed and optimized for the reading experience of Japanese novels and comics, featuring native support for right-to-left binding.

- **[User Guide](User-Guide)** — installation, reading, bookmarks, bookshelves, and settings.
- **[FAQ & Troubleshooting](FAQ-&-Troubleshooting)** — data locations, logs, and Linux tips.
- **[Developer Guide](Developer-Guide)** — architecture, development setup, and contribution workflow.
